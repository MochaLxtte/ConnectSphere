## Review decision in the recording

At **03:06–03:12**, Ryan says he will check the build, environment and results against the planned acceptance checks. At **03:14–03:29**, Irfan asks for feedback or unmet criteria. At **03:29–03:39**, members report that they have nothing further to raise. At **03:39–03:47**, Arvin asks whether the team accepts the stories against its Definition of Done. At **03:47–03:57**, the group responds affirmatively and Arvin says that the accepted stories and points will be recorded. He then identifies the release and submission follow-up. The opening identifies the reviewed bundle as 15 stories / 63 SP. The recorded decision applies to the bundle. [Sprint review](sprint-04/review.md) · [Release decision](../submission/release-record.md#release-decision)

In the revised written review, Ryan reports that every manual case on the review checklist passed and its individual results still needed writing up. Ernest confirms that aggregate outcome in the revised retrospective. At the corresponding point in the review video, Ryan discusses checking the evidence. Sampled frames across the recording show the Zoom speaker view, including during that exchange. Tristan’s separate confirmation identifies 75 passing procedures executed by their story owners. The written accounts give the overall result, the matched roster identifies the cases and executors, and the recording supplies the audible story decision.
