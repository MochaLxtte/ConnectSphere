# Sprint 3 planning

At planning on 4 October 2026, the team selected **13 stories / 63 SP** covering submission, assignment, review and venue arrangements. The forecast included five carried stories and five customer-change stories. Work that depended on equipment, registration, event changes or safety approval stayed in Sprint 4. ## Sprint goal

Finish the request and venue journey from organiser submission through Lead allocation, review and venue planning. The search results, suitability assessments, calendar and booking decisions should agree about the period occupied by an event, including its setup and turnaround. Holds and outages must affect the correct arrangements and notify the current coordinator. [Planning account, cues 03–04 and 26–39](evidence/meetings/planning.docx)

## Customer-change chronology

The team received the customer changes on **2 October**. At planning on **4 October**, the team selected **13 stories / 63 SP**. It grouped the Lead queue, buffers, outage recovery and holds in Sprint 3 and reserved independent venue actions and safety for Sprint 4. On **16 October**, the team reconstructed the allocation in the administrative records. The exports show the board as it stood during that reconciliation. ## Delivery and dependencies

Kai Jie would supply setup and turnaround fields, Arvin would apply them to the occupied interval, and Ryan and Irfan would use that interval for search and holds. Tristan and Ernest would connect submission, Lead assignment, reassignment and recipient changes. The sequence was to agree shared inputs, connect the feature checks, then exercise the entire journey ahead of the 17 October review. The account asks for independent reviewers and an integration coordinator without naming them at that point. Subsequent actual reviews appear beside the relevant stories.
