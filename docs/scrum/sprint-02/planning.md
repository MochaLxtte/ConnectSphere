## Sprint goal

Enable submitted requests to reach an assigned coordinator, and enable coordinators to identify suitable available venues from a maintained catalogue, request bookings, and receive Venue Staff decisions without conflicting bookings.

## Scope and selection

Irfan proposed a connected route from a submitted request to an assigned coordinator, suitable venue, booking request and Venue Staff decision without conflicts. Ryan asked that search and booking give consistent answers. The team kept the three repair stories at their original estimates. [Planning cues 4–15 and 29–35](evidence/meetings/planning.docx)

## Delivery and dependencies

The booking request, conflict decision and approval needed the same venue and time values. Calendar and search depended on the resulting availability. This dependency became a blocker at the 24 September standup. The follow-up clarified the fields by 26 September, but a full connected example still required work.
