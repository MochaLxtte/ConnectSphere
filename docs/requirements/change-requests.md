### CR-1 — Venue setup and turnaround time

**Original rule and customer change.** Week 1 identified preparation/reset as a possible consideration. G7's 25 August answer (row 22) instructed the team to assume no turnaround. The Sprint 2 rules used the requested interval to detect overlaps at submission and approval, while excluding other venues and non-overlapping periods. Week 7 item 1 requires configurable setup and turnaround for each venue so staff can actually prepare and reset it. A 10:00–12:00 booking with 30/45 minutes occupies 09:30–12:45. Existing bookings made problematic by the new rule must be identified. [G7 answers](sources/g7-customer-clarifications.xlsx) · [Original KAN-54-AC1–11](../scrum/sprint-02/sprint-backlog.md#kan-54--detect-venue-booking-conflicts)

**Refinement and design.** Kai Jie split staff configuration (2 SP) from enforcement across search, calendar, suitability, requests, approvals and blocks (8 SP). KAN-77-AC1–6 restrict editing to staff, validate whole minutes 0–1440, protect concurrent edits and return warnings. KAN-78-AC1–8 apply one half-open interval across those consumers. Touching occupied intervals can coexist. Buffer changes flag future conflicts while retaining existing booking identities. Migration 009 stores venue durations, 012 supplies occupied intervals, snapshots and warnings, and 013 extends the rule to holds. Arvin and Irfan reported on 8 and 10 October that they needed to settle shared interval inputs before the hold logic could use them. [KAN-77 criteria](../scrum/sprint-03/sprint-backlog.md#kan-77--configure-venue-setup-and-turnaround-durations) · [KAN-78 criteria](../scrum/sprint-03/sprint-backlog.md#kan-78--apply-venue-buffers-to-availability-and-booking-conflicts) · [Planning cues 16, 34–38, 40–42](../scrum/sprint-03/evidence/meetings/current/planning.docx) · [Standups 02–03](../scrum/sprint-03/evidence/meetings/current/daily.docx) · [Venue write sequence](../architecture/exports/seq-venue-write-locking.png)

**Verification.** S3-T18/T19 retain the original venue access and zero-buffer regressions alongside the new boundary cases. TC-08-20 and TC-14-06 have reported Pass results for warnings and preservation in `MAN-20261017-TEAM-01`. Later PostgreSQL tests exercised exact adjacency and one-minute buffer-only overlap in `AUTO-20261025-CLOSEOUT-02`; the subsequent warning-display repair has its own reported retest. ### CR-2 — Venue becomes unavailable after booking

**Original rule and customer change.** KAN-55 let Venue Staff save a block with a reason and exclude that period from planning. The original workflow refused a new operational block over a reserved booking. G7's 9 September rows 62/65 required blocking submission against reserved time but gave no later recovery procedure. Week 7 item 2 allows staff to record a real outage even after a booking has been approved. Venue Staff must inform the responsible coordinator, who can search for a replacement while retaining the event. [G7 answers](sources/g7-customer-clarifications.xlsx) · [Original KAN-55 and its acceptance checks](../scrum/sprint-02/sprint-backlog.md#kan-55--block-venue-for-operational-unavailability)

On 28 October the PO moved the readiness checks from KAN-81-AC9 to KAN-61-AC105 (confirmation) and KAN-84-AC101 (safety). Week 7 item 3 addresses conferences using several spaces at once. An auditorium and a breakout room each need their own suitability, availability and conflict checks. Changing one must preserve the others. Ryan sized the additional actions and failure handling at 5 SP because the team could reuse the booking workflow. [Original booking request](../scrum/sprint-02/sprint-backlog.md#kan-53--submit-venue-booking-request) · [Original staff decision](../scrum/sprint-02/sprint-backlog.md#kan-8--approve-or-reject-venue-booking-request) · [S3 planning cues 21, 44, 47–48](../scrum/sprint-03/evidence/meetings/current/planning.docx)

### CR-4 — Tentative venue holds expire

**Original rule and customer change.** Week 1 mentioned tentative holds during planning. On 25 August G7 confirmed that use, leaving duration and expiry unspecified (rows 25/34). KAN-54-AC10 treated ordinary pending requests as non-reserving. Week 7 item 4 adds a deadline so space cannot remain held indefinitely. The coordinator needs a notice before or at expiry and only an explicit decision can confirm the booking. [G7 answers](sources/g7-customer-clarifications.xlsx) · [Original pending-request rule](../scrum/sprint-02/sprint-backlog.md#kan-54--detect-venue-booking-conflicts)

**Refinement and design.** Irfan sized KAN-83 at 8 SP for the new state, timed release, notices and races. Under AC1–12 Venue Staff explicitly place a hold on an eligible pending arrangement. Its deadline must be later than the server clock and no later than the buffered occupied start. A live hold blocks the interval until approval, release or expiry. The coordinator receives the deadline at creation and a once-only outcome notice at expiry. A database-clock check excludes expired holds from availability even before cleanup. The API timer and scheduled database job call the same idempotent expiry routine. At review Ryan asked if expiry made the venue available. Irfan clarified that it removes only that hold's reservation: an existing booking or outage can still block the slot. [KAN-83 criteria](../scrum/sprint-03/sprint-backlog.md#kan-83--manage-temporary-venue-holds-with-expiry-and-automatic-release) · [Planning cues 36–42, 63, 70–72](../scrum/sprint-03/evidence/meetings/current/planning.docx) · [Review cues 09–10](../scrum/sprint-03/evidence/meetings/current/review.docx) · [Hold API](../architecture/api-contract.md#temporary-venue-holds-kan-83-cr-4)

**Verification.** S3-T26 and TC-14-13/15/16 cover expiry, replay and competing approval. `AUTO-20261021-KAN83-01` caught up overdue holds without duplicating history or notices. The initial manual scheduling attempt for TC-14-15 was blocked. On 25 October `OPS-20261025-HOLD-EXPIRY-01` observed successful shared database job ticks, and isolated database tests exercised the competing deadline orderings. A scheduler tick establishes that the function ran. It does not identify a real booking or inbox outcome. [G7 answers](sources/g7-customer-clarifications.xlsx) · [KAN-29 original criteria](../scrum/sprint-02/sprint-backlog.md#kan-29--automatically-assign-event-coordinator) · [Sprint 2 review, cue 03](../scrum/sprint-02/evidence/meetings/current/review.docx)

### CR-6 — Safety Officer and operational feasibility check

**Original rule and customer change.** KAN-36 covered the coordinator's initial event decision; KAN-52 checked venue suitability for advisory planning. The G7 customer confirmed on 25 August that failure to find a suitable venue would not itself reject an event (row 43). Week 7 item 6 adds a separately authorised operational check after venue and technical arrangements are confirmed and before preparation. A Safety Officer considers attendance, capacity/layout, emergency access, accessibility, equipment placement, crowd movement and restrictions. The officer may approve, reject or request changes. The customer permits the team to assume the officer always responds. [Original event decision](../scrum/sprint-01/sprint-backlog.md) · [Suitability criteria](../scrum/sprint-03/sprint-backlog.md#kan-52--check-venue-suitability) · [G7 answers](sources/g7-customer-clarifications.xlsx)

**Refinement and design.** KAN-84-AC1–12/101 requires authorised review against a specific arrangement revision. Requested changes identify venue, technical or both areas and require actual remediation before resubmission. Preparation needs a confirmed event, ready arrangements and current approval. The data model preserves immutable rounds and records preparation against the approved round. A safety-relevant change invalidates the previous approval. Ernest and Irfan initially sized screen/permission reuse at 5 SP. Kai Jie explained the role, version and integration tests. The Sprint 4 recording shows four 8s and two 5s at 10:00 and six 8s at 10:59, affirming the existing 8-SP estimate. On 22 October a missing equipment-state response blocked the connected check. On 24 October Kai Jie reported that he and Tristan had connected the response and could resume the connected checks.
